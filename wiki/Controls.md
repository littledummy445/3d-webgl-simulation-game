# Controls & Flight Mechanics

Pilot your exploration cruiser smoothly through deep space with intuitive keyboard controls.

## 🕹️ Keyboard Mapping

| Key | Action | Physics Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **W** | Thrust Forward | Accelerates cruiser forward along current heading |
| **S** | Reverse Thrust | Decelerates or moves cruiser backward |
| **A** | Steer Left | Rotates ship heading counter-clockwise |
| **D** | Steer Right | Rotates ship heading clockwise |
| **SPACE** | Ascend | Elevates cruiser upward on the Y-axis |
| **SHIFT** | Descend | Lowers cruiser downward on the Y-axis |

---
*Tip: Combine steering (`A`/`D`) with forward thrust (`W`) to navigate orbit paths around the Sun.*
