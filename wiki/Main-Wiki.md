# Planets, Moons & Orbits

The solar system simulation renders celestial bodies with proportional distances, procedural axial rotations, and dynamic time scaling.

## 🪐 Celestial Architecture

- **The Sun:** Central emissive point light source with a glowing corona mesh.
- **Inner & Outer Planets:** Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn (with rings), Uranus, and Neptune.
- **Moons:** Procedural lunar bodies orbiting Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.
- **Time Multiplier:** Controlled via the UI slider (`0.1x` to `5.0x`), scaling orbital speeds and rotation deltas in real-time.
